# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps build a positive community:

- being empathetic and kind to other people;
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving constructive feedback and accepting it gracefully;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.

## Enforcement

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers through a private report on GitHub
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the link) or by messaging a maintainer directly. The maintainers will review and
investigate every complaint promptly and fairly, and will respect the privacy of the person who reported it.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
